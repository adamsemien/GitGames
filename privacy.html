PrivacyWhat GitGames collects
The whole list

GitGames collects exactly three things. Nothing else.

  • Analytics events. Which screens you open, which levels you start and finish, which questions you miss, and which buttons you tap. No name, no email, no typed answers. Sent to PostHog only when analytics is switched on for this site.
  • Optional email. Only if you type it into the sign-up form after your third level. You can skip the form and nothing is sent. The address goes to the mailing list tool and nowhere else.
  • Local progress. Your XP, cleared levels, review pile and streak live in your own browser's storage. They never leave your device unless you copy the "Move progress" code yourself.
Payments

Pro is sold through a Stripe payment page. GitGames never sees your card. The unlock code you get back is checked in your browser and stored there.

Delete it

Tap "Reset all progress" on the home screen to wipe local progress. To be removed from the mailing list, use the unsubscribe link in any email.
